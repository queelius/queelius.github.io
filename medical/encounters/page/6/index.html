Clinical Encounters

This section contains detailed records of all medical appointments, hospital visits, and clinical consultations.

Subsections

  • Oncology Visits — Regular cancer treatment appointments
  • Hospital Admissions — Inpatient encounters
  • Specialist Consultations — Specialist evaluations and recommendations
  • Imaging Studies — Procedures for diagnostic imaging
  • Procedures — Medical and surgical procedures

Organization

Encounters are organized chronologically. Each encounter includes:

  • Date and time
  • Facility and provider
  • Chief complaint or reason for visit
  • Vital signs
  • Assessment and plan
  • Follow-up recommendations

Individual encounter records are available in the subdirectories.
