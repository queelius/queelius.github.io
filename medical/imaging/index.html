Imaging Reports

Radiological studies including CT, MRI, and other imaging

Imaging Reports

This section contains radiological reports and diagnostic imaging studies.

Imaging Types

CT (Computed Tomography)

  • CT Chest — Thoracic imaging
  • CT Abdomen/Pelvis — Abdominal and pelvic organs
  • Diagnostic CT — Staging and surveillance
  • CT with IV contrast — Enhanced visualization

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)

  • Brain MRI — Neuroimaging
  • Liver MRI — Hepatic lesion characterization

Ultrasound

  • Abdominal ultrasound — Hepatic and abdominal assessment
  • Vascular studies — Venous and arterial evaluation

Other Imaging

  • PET/CT — Positron emission tomography for metastatic disease
  • Bone scan — Skeletal metastasis assessment

Staging & Surveillance

Initial imaging for cancer staging established baseline disease extent. Surveillance imaging every 2-3 months monitors:

  • Response to chemotherapy
  • Progression or regression of metastases
  • Development of new lesions
  • Treatment effect on known lesions

Report Format

Each imaging report includes:

  • Study Type — What was imaged
  • Date — Study date
  • Technique — Imaging parameters
  • Findings — Detailed description of organs and lesions
  • Impression — Summary and clinical correlation
  • Comparison — To prior studies

Individual imaging reports are filed chronologically.
